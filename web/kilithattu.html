Kilithattu Kali

The Onam line-tag game, played at 20 ticks a second

Cross the lines

Four crossers, five defenders. Get past every line, step out the far side as uppu, then run home with the salt.

Pick a mode to begin.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys
  • Tab: switch crosser. As the Kili, hold a direction and you turn at the next junction.
  • Touch: drag on the field

Reading the field

  • Pacha, going out
  • Uppu, coming back
  • Guard, slides on its line
  • Kili, runs any line
  • Red box: pacha and uppu together, a foul

Rules in play

  • A defender within 0.6 m tags you, except in the home and far zones.
  • Each new line in order scores 1. Salt home scores 6 more. A round trip is 18.
  • Foul: the one who walked in is out. Leaving the side lines is out.
  • The innings lasts 120 s or until all four are out.
